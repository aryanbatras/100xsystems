## Problem

Count equal adjacent in ranges.

## Example

**Input:** abaa 2  
1 3  
2 4  

**Output:** 1 2  

## Solution Approach

### Method 1: Prefix sums

Precompute equal pairs.

## Time Complexity

O(n + q)

## Space Complexity

O(n)

## Edge Cases

- No equals

## Applications

- Range queries
