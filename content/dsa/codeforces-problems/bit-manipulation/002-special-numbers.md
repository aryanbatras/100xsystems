## Problem

Count numbers ≤ n that are special (powers of 2).

## Example

**Input:** 10  
**Output:** 4  

## Solution Approach

### Method 1: Count powers of 2

Find the highest power of 2 ≤ n, count from 1 to that.

## Time Complexity

O(log n)

## Space Complexity

O(1)

## Edge Cases

- n = 1: 1

## Applications

- Bit counting

## Practice Tips

- Use loop for powers
