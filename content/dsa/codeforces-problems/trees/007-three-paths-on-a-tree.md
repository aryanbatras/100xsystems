## Problem

Find three disjoint paths on tree to maximize sum of lengths.

## Example

**Input:** 3  
1 2  
2 3  
**Output:** 2  
1 2 3  

## Solution Approach

### Method 1: Find diameter

Find diameter, choose endpoints and another point.

## Time Complexity

O(n)

## Space Complexity

O(n)
