## Problem

Count blood cousins at depth k.

## Example

**Input:** 5 2  
1 2 3 4 5  
**Output:** 1  

## Solution Approach

### Method 1: LCA

Use LCA to find depths and parents.

## Time Complexity

O(n log n + q log n)

## Space Complexity

O(n log n)

## Edge Cases

- No cousins
