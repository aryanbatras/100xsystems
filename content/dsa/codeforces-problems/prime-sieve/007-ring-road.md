## Problem

Assign new roads inside or outside the ring without intersections.

## Example

**Input:** 4 2  
1 3  
2 4  
**Output:** io  

## Solution Approach

### Method 1: Check crossings

Assign based on order on the ring.

## Time Complexity

O(m^2)

## Space Complexity

O(m)

## Edge Cases

- No crossings

## Applications

- Planar graphs
