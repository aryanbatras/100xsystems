## Problem

Min operations to reach n from 1.

## Example

**Input:** 1 10  

**Output:** 6  

## Solution Approach

### Method 1: BFS

BFS from 1, operations as edges.

## Time Complexity

O(n)

## Space Complexity

O(n)

## Edge Cases

- n = 1

## Applications

- Shortest path
