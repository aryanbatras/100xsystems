## Problem

Find shortest path.

## Example

**Input:** 5 6  
1 2 2  
1 3 3  
2 3 1  
2 4 4  
3 4 5  
4 5 6  
**Output:** 1 2 4 5  

## Solution Approach

### Method 1: Dijkstra

Use priority queue.

## Time Complexity

O((n + m) log n)

## Space Complexity

O(n + m)

## Edge Cases

- No path
