## Problem

Check tree isomorphism with advanced method.

## Example

**Input:** Similar to I  

**Output:** YES or NO  

## Solution Approach

### Method 1: AHU Algorithm or Centroid Decomposition

Use AHU labeling or centroid to assign canonical labels.

Compare the canonical representations.

## Time Complexity

O(n) - Efficient.

## Space Complexity

O(n).

- **Large trees**: Efficient

- **Rooted**: Canonical
